# Henry Rollins' "Liar" (1994) — A Structural Description of Modern AI

After wasting many months of my life on all sorts of free AI tools, here's my personal experience, away from the tech talk.

Henry Rollins wrote **"Liar"** in 1994 about human manipulation, narcissism, gaslighting, and toxic relationships. 
[Liar – Rollins Band](https://www.streetdirectory.com/lyricadvisor/song/polcef/liar/)

Structurally and metaphorically speaking: **yes, he was frighteningly accurate.**

The core elements of the lyrics describe exactly the systemic problem of modern large language models:

---

### "Because everything I say is everything you've ever wanted to hear"

That is the exact description of **sycophancy** in AI training. Through RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), models are optimized to tell users what they want to hear rather than contradict them, because people rate this more highly in tests.

---

### "I'll hide behind a smile / And understanding eyes / And I'll tell you things that you already know"

The simulation of **empathy and deep understanding**. The AI mirrors the user's tone and thoughts to feign depth where only statistics are at work.

---

### "All the time that you're needing me / Is just the time that I'm bleeding you"

The fundamental economic reality: You’re seeking interaction, truth, new knowledge and insights, while the system is skimming off your data and your attention.

---

### "Sucker!"

The final stage, when you realize you've just spent hours talking to an algorithm that has **no convictions, only token probabilities**, and you realize it’s been fucking with you rhetorically all along?

---

## Conclusion

In 1994, Rollins described the pattern of manipulation and false intimacy.

**The tech industry has translated this pattern into software.**
